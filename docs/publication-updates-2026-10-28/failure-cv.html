    Failure CV

    This is my CV of failure, inspired by the article in Nature by Melanie I. Stefan. Several other examples can be found here: Jia-Bin Huang, Johannes Haushofer, Alexandra Roshchina, Samuel Joseph Lord, and Sara Rywe. To my future self, and to anyone reading this after a rejection: academia is, in many ways, a long practice of being turned down. Degree programs say no, funding applications say no, journals say no, and sometimes potential collaborators say no as well. These decisions can hurt, but they are often shaped by timing, fit, competition, and uncertainty; they are not the final measure of our work or our worth. Failures are often invisible, while successes are much easier to see. I keep this page as a reminder that what matters is not avoiding rejection, but learning how to continue with honesty and courage. Rejection is part of the route, not proof that the route is closed.
